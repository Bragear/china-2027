Travel plan · 3–4 weeks

China 2027

A 23-night route for four friends from Oslo, plus the alternatives we weighed. Independent travel, mid-range budget, no Mandarin.

The short version

  • Go, but plan around the visa. Visa-free entry for Norwegians is only confirmed through 31 Dec 2026. Assume a tourist visa for 2027 (about NOK 1,200 per person).
  • The plan: Beijing, Xi'an, Chongqing, Wulong, Zhangjiajie, Lijiang, Hong Kong, Wangxian Valley, Wuhan, Shanghai. 23 nights, in June or September.
  • Budget: roughly US$2,100–3,100 per person on the ground, US$400–650 for the two internal flights, plus US$700–1,300 for flights from Oslo.
  • Prep three things before flying: Alipay + WeChat Pay, a roaming eSIM, and a train booking account.

Five things that shape the trip

  1. The visa is the biggest planning risk. Any extension would be announced Nov–Dec 2026.
  2. High-speed rail is the backbone. But Guilin, Zhangjiajie and Yunnan are better reached by plane.
  3. Payments and internet are solved if set up correctly before arrival.
  4. Summer heat is the enemy. July–August is brutal in the east and south. June and September are far better.
  5. Four people means friction. Ride-hail cars seat four with no luggage room, and hotel rooms are built for two.

Our route · 23 nights

Beijing to Shanghai the long way

Beijing (3) → Xi'an (2) → Chongqing (3) → Wulong (1) → Zhangjiajie (3, Furong as a day trip) → Lijiang (2) → Hong Kong (3) → Wangxian Valley (1) → Wuhan (2) → Shanghai (3)

Pick a date and every slide fills in the days.

Why this one

The big icons (Great Wall, Terracotta Army, the Bund), Chongqing and the karst early, a cool highland break in Lijiang, Hong Kong as the pivot, then the Wangxian cliff town and Wuhan on the way to Shanghai. Fly home from Pudong.

Trade-offs

Two long flights (Zhangjiajie to Lijiang via Kunming, Lijiang to Hong Kong) and a full travel day from Hong Kong to Wangxian Valley. Ten stops in 23 nights means moving every second or third day. Night counts are our suggestion, not from the research.

Visa note

Hong Kong sits outside mainland immigration, so this is two mainland entries. The second one is short, about six nights, which makes the 30-day count easy. Confirm with the embassy before relying on it.

Alternatives we weighed

Four other ways to do it

Classic + Karst · 24 nights

Beijing → Xi'an → Chengdu → Guilin/Yangshuo → Zhangjiajie → Shanghai + Hangzhou

The research's original pick. Adds pandas, Sichuan food and the Li River. Drops Hong Kong and Yunnan.

Grand tour via Shangri-La · 26 nights

Beijing → Shanghai → Wuhan → Wangxian Valley → Hong Kong → Zhangjiajie → Furong → Wulong → Chongqing → Kunming → Lijiang → Shangri-La

Our stops in reverse, plus Kunming, Furong and Shangri-La. Ends in the Tibetan highlands, so you fly home from Kunming.

Mountains, Rivers & Yunnan · 23 nights

Zhangjiajie → Fenghuang → Guilin/Yangshuo → Kunming → Dali → Lijiang → Shangri-La

The anti-city itinerary. Sits at altitude, so it is the choice if dates land in July–August.

Food & Culture · 22 nights

Xi'an → Chengdu → Chongqing → Guangzhou → Shanghai + Hangzhou

Four of China's five best food cities. Markets and cooking classes instead of monuments. The cheapest option.

Visa for Norwegians

Now, to 31 Dec 2026

Visa-free for up to 30 days of tourism.

2027

No extension announced. Only Russia has been extended. China has extended twice before, each time around 1 December.

Decision rule

If nothing is published by 1 Dec 2026, apply for an L visa at the Oslo centre in Lysaker. About NOK 1,200 per person, 4 working days.

The 30-day cap matters. The waiver is not extendable in-country. If the trip approaches 30 days, get the visa regardless. Even a one-day overstay causes trouble on departure.

Practicalities

Payments

Alipay and WeChat Pay both take foreign Visa and Mastercard. Set up both. Purchases under ¥200 are fee-free, above that a 3% fee. Carry ¥500–1,000 cash.

Internet

Google, WhatsApp, Instagram and YouTube are blocked. A roaming eSIM (Airalo, Holafly) bypasses this without a VPN. Install a VPN before arriving as backup.

Hotels

Book on Trip.com. Three-star and up almost always take foreigners. Message the hotel after booking to confirm.

English

Limited outside big hotels and airports. Baidu Translate, Youdao and Microsoft Translator work without a VPN. DiDi removes the taxi language barrier.

When to go

Avoid

  • Chinese New Year, 6 Feb 2027 and the week around it
  • Labour Day, around 1–5 May
  • National Day Golden Week, 1–7 Oct

July–August

Brutally hot and humid in Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an, Chengdu and Chongqing, plus typhoon risk on the coast. Only highland Yunnan stays pleasant.

Best windows

Our route: June or September. Lijiang is the only cool stop, so July–August would be hard. The Yunnan alternative is the true summer option.

Budget per person

Mid-range, sharing twin rooms, excluding flights from Oslo. About US$90–150 per day. Our route's figure is an estimate scaled from the research; Hong Kong is pricier than the mainland cities.

Travelling as four

  • Rooms: Chinese hotels are built for two. Plan on two twin rooms. Hostels have four-bed private rooms.
  • Ride-hailing: a standard DiDi seats four with no room for luggage. Expect two cars or a larger vehicle, especially for airport transfers.
  • Trains and flights: book all four seats in one transaction to sit together.
  • What scales well: hotpot dinners, tuk-tuk food tours, a private Great Wall car, bamboo rafting, cooking classes, bike rentals.

The plan

Our route, in June or September 2027

  1. Now to mid-2026: pick dates, price flights, book open-jaw into Beijing and out of Shanghai.
  2. December 2026: check the visa-free extension. If none, book visa appointments in Oslo. Book international flights.
  3. 2–3 months before: book the two internal flights (Zhangjiajie to Lijiang via Kunming, Lijiang to Hong Kong) and all hotels on Trip.com. Two twin rooms each.
  4. 1 month before: register on 12306, buy the eSIM, install a VPN, download Alipay, WeChat, DiDi, Trip.com and a translator.
  5. 15 days before each leg: book high-speed rail the moment sales open, especially Beijing–Xi'an, Xi'an–Chongqing and Wuhan–Shanghai.

Our route, week by week

Week 1

Beijing 3 · Xi'an 2 · Chongqing 2

Fly Oslo to Beijing. Forbidden City, Mutianyu Great Wall, hutong food, Peking duck. Train to Xi'an: Terracotta Army, city wall by bike, Muslim Quarter. Train on to Chongqing: Hongya Cave, hotpot, the cable car.

Week 2

Chongqing 1 · Wulong 1 · Zhangjiajie 3 · Lijiang 2

Ciqikou or Dazu, then train to Wulong for the Three Natural Bridges. Train to Zhangjiajie: the Avatar pillars, Tianmen Mountain, a Furong day trip. Fly via Kunming to Lijiang: old town and the snow mountain.

Week 3

Hong Kong 3 · Wangxian Valley 1 · Wuhan 2 · Shanghai 1

Fly to Hong Kong: the Peak, Star Ferry, dim sum, a hike. Long rail day to Shangrao and the Wangxian cliff town at night. Train to Wuhan: Yellow Crane Tower, East Lake. Train to Shanghai.

Final days

Shanghai 2

The Bund, Yu Garden, French Concession, Shanghai Tower. Fly home from Pudong. 23 nights in total.

If things change

  • Dates must be July–August: switch to the Yunnan alternative for the cooler highlands, or accept heat everywhere except Lijiang.
  • Hard three weeks: drop Wangxian Valley and Wuhan and fly Hong Kong to Shanghai direct. About 20 nights.
  • Visa-free is extended: keep the trip under 30 days and skip the visa, but count days carefully.

Prices are 2026 observations. Holiday dates for 2027 are confirmed only when the State Council issues its notice, around Nov 2026. Re-check embassy notices before booking anything non-refundable.
